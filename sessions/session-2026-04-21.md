## What I understood
* **Transactions** are used to group multiple CRUD requests from a client into a single, reliable unit of work.
* To maintain database integrity, transactions rely on **ACID properties**:
    * **Atomicity:** Treats all updates as one unit; they either all succeed or all fail ("all-or-nothing").
    * **Consistency:** Ensures the database safely moves from one valid state to another.
    * **Isolation:** Prevents race conditions (often using locks) so concurrent transactions don't interfere, providing serializability.
    * **Durability:** Guarantees that completed data changes will survive system crashes and restarts.
* **Autocommit** is a setting that, when enabled, automatically treats every individual SQL statement as its own complete transaction.
* **Savepoints** act as internal checkpoints within a larger transaction, allowing you to undo or roll back a specific portion of the work without having to cancel the entire transaction.
