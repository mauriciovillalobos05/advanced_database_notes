## Topics covered

## What I understood
- OLTP systems are optimized for handling many real-time transactions efficiently and reliably.
- OLTP databases usually store recent operational data and use normalized schemas to reduce redundancy.
- OLAP systems are optimized for analyzing large amounts of historical data and generating reports.
- OLAP databases mainly perform read-heavy analytical queries instead of transactional operations.
- Star schemas improve analytical query performance by organizing data into fact and dimension tables.
- Fact tables store measurable business metrics and connect to dimension tables through foreign keys.
- Dimension tables provide descriptive information that helps filter and group analytical data.
- ETL pipelines move data from OLTP systems into OLAP systems through extraction, transformation, and loading processes.
- OLTP and OLAP serve different business purposes:
    - OLTP supports daily operations.
    - OLAP supports decision-making and analytics.
- Denormalization in OLAP improves performance for complex aggregations and reporting queries.
